# Journey: Ledger Performance

## Job story

When a business needs accountable paid growth, it wants to know whether a specialist can translate media spend into clear next decisions; therefore the page makes capability, proof and a human contact path easy to scan.

## IA

Home / Profile / Capabilities / Outcomes / Contact.

## Primary flow

1. Land on the proposition: make every click accountable.
2. Scan trusted tools and the 30% CPA result.
3. Understand the operator and four practical capabilities.
4. Read the outcome evidence and begin a WhatsApp conversation.

## Page specs

### Landing page

- Header: sparse anchor navigation plus a direct contact action.
- Hero: proposition, location/role metadata, and a single primary CTA.
- Proof strip: certifications and existing CPA claim, no invented outcomes.
- Profile: expertise and personal framing.
- Capabilities: ordered operational services, each with the job it performs.
- Outcomes: one quantified result plus supporting qualitative evidence.
- Contact: unambiguous WhatsApp action and persistent WhatsApp affordance.

### Responsive rules

The desktop ledger collapses to one column below 800px. Every touch action remains at least 44px tall; the floating WhatsApp action clears the footer with reserved bottom padding.

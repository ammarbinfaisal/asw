# JOURNEY.md — Conversion Studio

For visual tokens and surface rules, see [DESIGN.md](./DESIGN.md).

## Job

**Job story (Moesta):** When paid acquisition feels expensive or opaque, I want to assess a credible PPC specialist quickly, so I can start a low-friction conversation about more accountable growth.

**Functional job:** Understand expertise, services, evidence, and contact route.  
**Emotional job:** Feel informed rather than sold to.  
**Social job:** Make a defensible specialist choice for the business.

**Four forces:**

- Push: wasted spend, unclear tracking, or underperforming campaigns.
- Pull: measurable optimization across Google Ads, GTM, and GA4.
- Anxiety: uncertainty about proof, fit, and what the engagement involves.
- Habit: staying with familiar campaign setups even when performance stalls.

## IA and decision model

This is a one-page task-oriented structure with global anchor navigation: About, Services, Results, Contact. The visitor may move between exploration and evaluation rather than following a strict funnel (Google messy middle, 2020), so proof, capability, and contact remain easy to revisit.

The page uses the StoryBrand SB7 relationship: the visitor is the hero trying to improve performance; Abdul is the experienced guide. The sequence also follows Shapiro’s conversion equation by raising desire with proof while reducing labor and confusion with clear services and a direct WhatsApp contact.

## Primary task flow

1. Arrive from search, referral, or direct link.
2. Read the accountable-growth claim and scan the 30% CPA proof.
3. Validate tools/certifications and the specialist’s background.
4. Compare the four service capabilities.
5. Review impact and testimonial.
6. Choose “Get a free consultation” or the WhatsApp action.
7. Exit to WhatsApp with a prefilled, editable message.

Failure recovery: if WhatsApp is unavailable, the footer retains the public website route. Anchor navigation works without JavaScript.

## Page spec — home

**Purpose:** Help a prospective client confidently decide whether to start a PPC consultation.

**Entry points:** Search, referral, direct URL, external profile.

**Content blocks, in order:**

1. Header — identity and four anchor links.
2. Hero — direct outcome claim, Google Ads/analytics scope, consultation CTA, and 30% CPA proof.
3. Proof rail — Google Ads certification, GTM, GA4, and worldwide availability.
4. About / guide — portrait, experience, and working style.
5. Services — four concrete capabilities with numbered studio labels.
6. Impact — 30% CPA reduction, conversion improvement, and Fullstacktics testimonial.
7. Contact — expectation-setting copy and free-consultation/WhatsApp action.
8. Footer — ownership, website, and existing external links.

**States:** Static default. External chat failure is recoverable through the website link.  
**Primary CTA:** “Get a free consultation” → `#contact`, then WhatsApp.  
**Exit:** WhatsApp or abdulsamadwani.com.

## Ethical persuasion check

- Transparency: consultation and WhatsApp destinations are explicit.
- Accuracy: only the existing 30% CPA claim, certifications, and named testimonial are used.
- Symmetry: there is no forced flow or punished decline.
- User interest: content explains fit before asking for contact.
- Reversibility: opening a chat does not submit or purchase anything.
- Voluntary: no countdown, scarcity, FOMO, or manufactured urgency.

# Conversion Studio — design brief

**Status:** confirmed  
**Confirmation basis:** The user explicitly requested four implemented frontend explorations and named typography, color, and layout as the axes to plan. This branch is the warm, conversion-oriented exploration.

## Audience and job

Growth-minded founders and marketing leads need to decide whether Abdul Samad Wani can make Google Ads spend more accountable. They need a fast read on expertise, services, evidence, and how to start a conversation.

## Existing truth to preserve

- Abdul Samad Wani is a PPC and growth strategy specialist.
- Services cover Google Ads/PPC management, strategic growth planning, GTM/GA4 optimization, and integrated marketing.
- Existing proof includes Google Ads certification, GTM and GA4 capability, a stated 30% CPA reduction, and the Fullstacktics testimonial.
- Primary action is a free consultation; WhatsApp is the direct contact channel.
- Existing portrait and Google product assets remain in use.

## Direction

“Conversion Studio”: warm and energetic, but disciplined and credible. A sunlit cream ground, vermilion action color, and inky neutral should feel like a strategist’s working studio rather than a generic SaaS landing page. Typography combines a friendly humanist system sans for reading with condensed, emphatic display type.

The signature move is an offset print frame: proof cards and small labels use a hard ink or vermilion offset instead of gradients or glass effects.

## Ethical constraints

- Use only existing, attributable proof; do not invent client counts, spend managed, or urgency.
- No countdowns, scarcity, confirmshaming, inflated social proof, or coercive CTA language.
- The consultation action stays voluntary, direct, and reversible.

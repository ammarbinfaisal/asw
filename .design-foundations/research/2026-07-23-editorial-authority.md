# Research: Editorial Authority

**Date:** 2026-07-23  
**Status:** confirmed from the supplied direction and existing site content

## Brief

Reframe Abdul Samad Wani’s Google Ads portfolio as a high-trust independent consultancy. Preserve the existing claims, services, certification assets, 30% CPA result, testimonial, WhatsApp contact path, and worldwide positioning while making the page feel more strategic and authored.

## Audience and job

The primary visitor is a founder or marketing lead comparing PPC specialists. They need to understand the offer, find proof, and start a low-friction conversation without decoding marketing theatre.

**Job story (Klement):** When paid acquisition is expensive or opaque, I want an expert to connect campaign decisions to trustworthy measurement, so I can invest with confidence.

## Design implications

- Archetype: Sage primary, Ruler secondary.
- Family: Editorial Minimalism.
- Grounding: a strategy journal’s information discipline + a financial broadsheet’s typographic authority.
- Structure register: calm, precise, generous; expressive moments are limited to the hero and oversized section markers.
- Typography: screen-safe Georgia display at large sizes only; Arial/system sans for body and UI.
- Color: warm paper neutrals, deep ink, one muted copper accent generated from hue 42; no gradients or glass effects.
- Composition: asymmetric editorial columns, hairline rules, generous whitespace, one compact metric strip.
- Signature move: oversized vertical section numbers that sit outside the main reading column on wide screens.

## Content inventory

Hero value proposition; Google Ads, GTM, GA4 and CPA trust signals; founder bio; four service areas; two outcome statements; one client testimonial; consultation CTA; WhatsApp contact; website and LinkedIn links.

## Risks and answers

- The current copy is broad: use hierarchy and labeling to make it scannable without inventing claims.
- Certification icons can feel like a generic logo bar: treat them as restrained evidence in a metric strip.
- Copper can lose contrast: use the palette’s dark `accent-on-solid` token on copper fills.
- Editorial display type can impair reading: restrict serif to headings and large numerals; body remains sans.

## Taste signals

Light, high-trust, strategic, off-white paper, deep ink, selective saffron/copper, asymmetric editorial composition, generous whitespace, metric strip, oversized vertical section markers.
